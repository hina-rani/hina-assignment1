# Introduction
In the era of artificial intelligence, even simple games can be transformed into intelligent, adaptive, and personalized experiences. The "Click the Button" game leverages AI tools to analyze player behavior, adjust difficulty dynamically, and provide tailored feedback. By integrating AI, the game becomes more than just a test of speed and accuracy—it becomes a smart, interactive experience that evolves with the player.

# How it wroks

Start Game: Click the "Start Game" button to begin.

Click the Button: Click the "Click Me!" button as many times as you can within 10 seconds.

Game Over: After 10 seconds, the game ends, and a message box displays your total clicks.
